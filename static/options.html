rtcstats WebRTC monitor

On the sites you enable, the extension records the WebRTC API calls and getStats() results and streams them to your rtcstats server. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Server

Starts with ws:// or wss://.

Sent as rtcstats-token when the server requires authorization. Stored in this browser only, never shown to the sites you trace.

Added to every dump, to find your traces on the server.

Traced sites

Add the site of your meeting platform. A platform that runs the call in an embedded frame from another site needs that site too.
